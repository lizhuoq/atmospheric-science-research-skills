# Atmospheric-science comparison checks

## Measurement and observation

Check calibration, retrieval assumptions, detection limits, representativeness, sampling frequency, missingness, quality flags, station siting, and collocation. Distinguish a retrieved geophysical variable from a direct measurement.

## Models and forecasts

Record model version, grid and effective resolution, vertical coordinates, initialization, boundary conditions, parameterizations, forcing scenario, ensemble construction, lead time, and verification data. Separate hindcast skill, forecast skill, process fidelity, and projection spread.

## Statistics and machine learning

Identify the estimand, sampling unit, dependence, train/validation/test separation, leakage risk, baseline, uncertainty interval, multiple testing, calibration, and out-of-distribution limits. Predictive importance is not causal effect.

## Cross-study comparability

Reconcile units; sign conventions; UTC versus local or solar time; accumulation interval; instantaneous versus averaged fields; pressure, height, and model levels; regridding; area weighting; land/ocean masks; climatological baseline; anomaly definition; and spatial/temporal aggregation.

## Causal claims

Require an explicit identification argument, intervention or defensible quasi-experiment, assumptions, confounder handling, and sensitivity analysis. Mechanistic plausibility or temporal ordering alone does not identify a causal effect.
